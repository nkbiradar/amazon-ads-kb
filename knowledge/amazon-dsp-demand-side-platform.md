# Amazon DSP Demand Side Platform

## Overview

Amazon DSP (Demand-Side Platform) is an omnichannel marketing solution that delivers results through relevancy at scale, providing choice and flexibility to drive meaningful moments between brands and consumers. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## What Makes Amazon DSP Different

### Premium Supply
Reach relevant customers across the largest supply of premium, ad-supported content wherever they're spending time across Amazon-exclusive properties like Prime Video, and across third-party supply through direct integrations with publishers and leading supply-side platforms. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Unique Audience Signals
Gain better understanding of audiences with unique signals powered by expansive browsing, shopping, and streaming insights. Amazon DSP helps advertisers reach qualified users using deterministic and modeled audiences without relying on traditional ad identifiers. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### AI-Powered Optimization
Maximize return on investment with AI-powered solutions that leverage first- and third-party insights to automate tasks and target relevant audiences on Amazon and across third-party apps, sites, and devices. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Interoperable Technology
For efficient end-to-end campaign management, interoperability between systems (Amazon Web Services, Amazon Marketing Cloud, Amazon Publisher Cloud) eliminates fragmentation in planning, buying, and measurement. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Advertising Capabilities

### Ad Formats Available
Engage audiences with a combination of audio, display, online video, Streaming TV, and physical store advertising across all formats and devices. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Placement Opportunities
Your ads can appear in:
- Amazon Originals on Prime Video
- Livestreams on Twitch  
- Live sports including Thursday Night Football
- Amazon.com and Amazon properties
- Amazon Fresh kiosks
- Connected devices (Fire TV, Kindle, Alexa)
- Thousands of premium third-party sites and apps via Amazon Publisher Direct and leading third-party exchanges [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Targeting Capabilities

Amazon DSP offers audience targeting in three main categories:
1. **Amazon Audiences**: Based on first-party buying, browsing, and streaming signals
2. **Advertiser Audiences**: Driven by custom inputs like product relevance and remarketing
3. **Third-Party Audiences**: Sourced from broader internet activities [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Programmatic Buying Options

Amazon DSP offers:
- Real-time bidding (RTB)
- Private marketplaces (PMP)
- Private auction
- Programmatic guaranteed buying
- Deals-based options with centralized inventory hub [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)

## Measurement and Reporting

### Campaign Metrics
Campaign reporting includes industry-standard metrics and Amazon-proprietary metrics such as:
- Detail page view rate (DPVR)
- Add-to-list (ATL) counts
- New-to-brand (NTB) metrics
- Reach, frequency, and viewability metrics [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Advanced Measurement
- Omnichannel Metrics (OCM)
- Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)
- Custom analytics using event-level data in Amazon Marketing Cloud
- Third-party measurement solutions (brand lift studies, offline sales lift) [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp)
