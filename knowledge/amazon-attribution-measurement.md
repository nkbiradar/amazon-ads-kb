# Amazon Attribution Tracking and Measurement

## Overview

Amazon Attribution is a free advertising and analytics measurement solution that provides insights into the on-Amazon impact of marketing strategies, helping advertisers understand how non-Amazon marketing channels drive sales on Amazon. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## What is Amazon Attribution?

Amazon Attribution enables advertisers to track the performance of marketing channels that drive traffic to Amazon, providing comprehensive insights into how external advertising impacts Amazon sales performance. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## How Amazon Attribution Works

### Tag-Based Tracking
Amazon Attribution uses attribution tags placed in destination URLs to track customer journeys from external touchpoints through to Amazon purchases. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Integration with Amazon Ads API
Amazon Attribution is available in the Amazon Ads API, enabling programmatic access to metrics within the tool and streamlined measurement activities. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Implementation Process

### Getting Started
1. **Enable Amazon Attribution** in your Amazon Ads account
2. **Generate attribution tags** for your marketing channels
3. **Add tags to destination URLs** in your external campaigns
4. **Monitor performance** through Amazon Attribution dashboard
5. **Optimize based on insights** from attribution data [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Tag Configuration
Attribution tags are tracking parameters added to destination URLs that connect external clicks to Amazon purchases. Each marketing channel gets unique tags for proper attribution. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/basics-of-amazon-attribution" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->
