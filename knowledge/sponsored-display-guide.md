# Sponsored Display Guide

## Overview

Sponsored Display helps reach more desired customers and re-engage existing ones, boosting performance throughout the purchase journey from awareness to purchase to repurchase. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## What is Sponsored Display?

Sponsored Display is a digital advertising format that uses visual elements like images, graphics, animation, or video to promote products, services, or brands on websites, applications, or social media platforms. Unlike search-based advertising, Sponsored Display uses audience-based customer shopping behavior for targeting rather than keywords. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Key Benefits

Sponsored Display can help you:
- Reach new audiences and increase awareness of specific products
- Improve visibility across your entire product catalog
- Boost sales of high-performing products on Amazon
- Accelerate sales of high-price or long-sales-cycle products
- Promote clearance and seasonal items
- Cross-sell related products on your own pages to expand audience reach during major shopping events [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Eligibility Requirements

Sponsored Display is available for vendors or professional sellers who have completed Amazon brand registry. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Pricing Models

Sponsored Display has no minimum spend requirement. Two cost types are available:

### Cost-Per-Click (CPC)
Pay when customers click your ad. Final CPC based on adjusted bid and other factors.

### Viewable Cost-Per-Thousand (vCPM)
Pay when your ad receives 1,000 viewable impressions. Final vCPM based on adjusted bid and other factors. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Campaign Setup

### Budget Configuration
- No minimum spend requirement
- Set appropriate daily budget
- Recommended daily budget for small/medium businesses: $40–60 (or local currency equivalent)
- Can update budget anytime based on performance [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Bid Optimization Options
Three bid optimization options are available:

1. **Optimize for Viewable Impressions**: Optimizes bids to help increase ad impressions, likely resulting in higher viewable impressions and lower click rates compared to other optimization options.

2. **Optimize for Page Visits**: Shows ads to shoppers more likely to click, helping improve purchase intent. System optimizes bids for higher click rates.

3. **Optimize for Conversions**: Shows ads to shoppers more likely to purchase, helping drive new customer sales and repeat purchases. Recommended for first campaigns. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Targeting Options

### Content-Related Targeting
Target specific products or related product categories on Amazon to help shoppers find your products. Can refine using price, brand, and Prime shipping eligibility. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

**Where ads appear**: Amazon site ad placements including product detail pages, next to customer reviews, and shopping search results pages.

### Audience Targeting
Use Sponsored Display audiences to reach customers on Amazon and third-party destinations based on interests related to your products and brand. Can re-engage customers who have browsed or purchased your products or similar products on Amazon. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

**Audience Categories**:
- **Amazon Audiences**: Pre-built segments based on first-party shopping insights, streaming insights, and entertainment industry insights
- **Remarketing Browse**: Re-engage audiences who browsed your products or similar products on Amazon but haven't purchased
- **Remarketing Purchase**: Re-engage audiences who purchased your products or related products on Amazon to drive repeat purchases and cross-sell

**Where ads appear**: Both Amazon sites (homepage, product detail pages) and third-party destinations (Twitch, third-party apps and websites) [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

## Ad Formats and Creative

### Automatic Creative Generation
Sponsored Display automatically generates ads using:
- Product images from your product detail pages
- Product title, star ratings, Prime badges, and pricing
- Automatic updates if product detail page information changes [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Custom Creative Options
Consider using custom brand logos, headlines, and lifestyle images to tailor ads and highlight your brand and unique selling points. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T18:55:27Z" -->

### Product Selection
- Add at least 10 performing products to campaigns for optimal results
- Choose products that are same category, related, similar price range, have positive customer reviews
- Include at least four high-quality, scalable images
- Add at least three bullet points describing product advantages [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)

## Optimization Strategies

### 5 Ways to Improve Performance

1. **Add 10+ ASINs to your Sponsored Display campaigns** - More products mean more auction participation and higher relevance to broader customer groups.

2. **Ensure sufficient budget to keep ads running all day** - Campaigns that frequently hit daily budget caps may miss potential impression opportunities.

3. **Try different campaign targets** - Targeting settings significantly impact results. Refine targeting rather than just lowering bids for underperforming categories.

4. **Consider sales of non-promoted products** - If ads generate sales of non-promoted products, consider promoting those products too.

5. **Leverage high-performing audience segments** - If a specific audience segment performs well, try similar segments or create parallel campaigns with different targeting. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)

## Performance Timeline

Recommend waiting at least two weeks after launching campaigns to have sufficient data for evaluation. Once you have sufficient campaign metrics, review weekly to make data-driven decisions for improvement. [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)

## Reporting Options

### Available Reports
- **Purchased Product Reports**: Understand which non-promoted products customers purchased after clicking your ads
- **Placement Reports**: Performance metrics about your targeted audiences or products
- **Advertised Product Reports**: Sales and performance metrics for promoted products across campaigns
- **Campaign Reports**: Detailed analysis of Sponsored Display campaign performance [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide)
