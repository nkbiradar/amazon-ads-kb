# Negative Keywords And Product Targeting

## Overview

- Negative keywords support phrase and exact match types [¹](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative phrase match has maximum 4 words per keyword and 80 character limit [²](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative exact match has maximum 10 words per keyword and 80 character limit [³](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative keyword match type cannot be changed after creation [⁴](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative keywords require 72 hours buffer period to take effect [⁵](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative products/ASINs require 96 hours buffer period to take effect [⁶](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Negative product targets prevent ads from displaying when ASIN matches the target [⁷](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Ad group level negative product targets affect product attribute or automatic targets in the same ad group [⁸](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->

- Campaign level negative product targets affect product attribute or automatic targets in any ad group within the campaign [⁹](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W)
<!-- provenance: source_url="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GTEHPEG5BXY9UX5W" source_type="official" confidence="high" last_checked="2026-08-13T20:13:15Z" -->
